## Precision and interpretation

These runs continue the original BF16 backbone training recipe. FP32 parameter/optimizer-state training controls have **not** been run. The checkpoint audit found normalization weights unchanged at their initial value despite nonzero Adam moments; see `first_seed_results/depth_diagnostic.json`. Depth curves are observations under this recipe and do not establish an architectural depth-scaling advantage.

The parameter ladder and older 600M/1B evaluations remain separated by training corpus in the six-panel plot. This snapshot does not fit a pooled scaling exponent across those corpora.

## Recovery

Two Dense data-budget jobs failed on 2026-10-02 with `Disk quota exceeded` during checkpoint writes. On 2026-10-04, their last valid checkpoints were copied to local scratch and resumed at updates 2,251/6,000 and 751/12,000, preserving optimizer state and consumed-token offsets. Project quota currently has approximately 38 GiB of room below its soft limit.

The local dispatcher previously transferred only never-started pending jobs, leaving local GPUs idle once all remaining jobs had started or failed remotely. It now skips remote completions and can copy and validate a held pending requeue before cancelling that array element and continuing locally. Running remote jobs are left running. Fourteen focused execution checks pass, including release after a staging failure, preservation of optimizer state, and refusal to transfer a running job.
